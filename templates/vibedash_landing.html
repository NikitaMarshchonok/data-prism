What changed in our SaaS metrics—and what should we investigate?

  1. Prepare comparable periods. Use a CSV with one consistent row unit, a date, and numeric business metrics such as revenue, customer count, or an already calculated churn rate. Include plan or region only if available.
  2. Check the evidence. Review data-quality warnings, trends, and supporting numbers. A relationship is not proof of its cause; metric definitions and denominators still need your review.
  3. Choose one next action. In the result, track a priority with an owner, a success metric, and a review date. Return to Decisions to record what actually happened.

Optional pilot measurement & privacy

Unchecked by default and chosen separately for each run. Analysis works without opting in. Measurement records contain a job ID, a keyed pseudonymous guest-browser or pilot-account scope token, demo/upload type, milestone timestamps, and optional fixed-choice feedback. They do not contain source rows, filenames, prompts, IP addresses, or decision text. These measurements stay on this service; no third-party analytics tracker is used.

This applies only to pilot measurement. The analysis itself temporarily stores your uploaded data and results; decision cases store the evidence summary and text you provide. Measurement records expire 30 days after a run starts and are removed during subsequent application activity. The service keeps at most 10,000 measurement records. Demo activity is reported separately from uploads.
