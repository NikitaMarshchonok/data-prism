Danger zone

Delete pilot account

This is permanent for the account record. To continue, enter your current password and type DELETE exactly.

What to expect

  • Deletion is blocked while this account has queued or running jobs. Wait for them to finish, then retry.
  • Account-owned jobs, decision cases, opt-in pilot measurements, and server artifacts that can be safely identified as this account's are purged.
  • Classic analysis and monitoring data are not account-owned by this action.
  • Local copies and downloads, plus historical offline backups, are not erased. Free-host storage may disappear earlier after a restart or redeploy.
  • Deletion is not cross-store atomic; unidentifiable orphan files may remain until normal retention cleanup.

Enter the uppercase word DELETE. This browser check does not replace server-side validation.

Pilot account notes

Password recovery requires a saved one-time code. There is no email recovery or email verification in this pilot, and support cannot bypass a missing code.

Free-host storage is ephemeral and may be erased after a restart or redeploy. Keep your own copy of important results.
